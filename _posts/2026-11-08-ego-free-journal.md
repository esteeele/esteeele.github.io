I made a [mini-project](/ego-free-journal.html) where words you type disappear after a short period of time. I had the idea after reading something (I can't remember where) which said that having everything recorded was giving us an outsized self of self and this was actually making us less happy. The reasoning being we can control a lot, but not everything, so that when things out of control don't go our way we feel an oversized psychological wound.  

So this is just a zen art project that imagines what a journal would be like if we had no sense of self, like if Shakespeare had written Hamlet just for himself then watched is dissolve away.
